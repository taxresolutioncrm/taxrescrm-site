Transcripts

Transcript analysis

Import IRS account transcripts and get balances with accruals, CSED estimates, and automatic flags for liens, levies, unfiled SFRs, and installment or CNC status — parsed in seconds, not read line by line.

Forms

IRS & state form generation

Pre-filled 2848s, 8821s, and 433-series forms straight from the client file, plus state POAs with cover pages — sent for e-signature in the same motion.

Calling

Built-in phone, SMS & fax

A real multi-agent phone system inside the CRM: inbound routing, IVR, voicemail, recordings, and AI call recaps — every call logged to the client file automatically.

Scheduling

Client self-scheduling

Clients book their own consultations from a public link — reminders, reschedules, and calendar sync included. You send a link; the calendar fills itself.

Money

Payments & installment billing

Charge cards, save payment methods, send payment links, and run installment plans — with receivables that reconcile themselves against invoices.

Clients

Client portal & e-signature

A branded portal where clients sign agreements, complete financial intake, upload documents, and see status — every action written back to their file as a note.
