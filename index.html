How a Case Moves

From first call to resolved case — everything connected.

TaxRes CRM is organized around the client file, not disconnected modules. Every action stays attached to the matter it belongs to.

01

Lead comes in

Inbound call, booked appointment, or web form — a lead record is created automatically with the full communication history.

02

Case opens

Convert to a client. Transcripts are imported, the 2848 is pre-filled and sent for e-signature, and the intake form goes to the client portal.

03

Resolution work

Tasks, deadlines, IRS correspondence, notices, and calls all live in one place. Workflows handle the follow-ups. Nothing slips.

04

Case closes

Invoice sent, payment collected, resolution documented. The full case history remains in the file. Time and billing reconciled.
