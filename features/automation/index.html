The Problem

Repetitive work accumulates faster than firms realize

A resolution practice runs on process. Every Offer in Compromise engagement follows a similar path. Every Installment Agreement has a predictable sequence. Every new client needs the same intake documents, authorization forms, and initial follow-up. When those processes aren't automated:

  • Staff manually create the same tasks for every new case.
  • Follow-up emails are sent late — or not at all — because someone forgot.
  • Document requests are tracked in spreadsheets or to-do lists.
  • Status updates happen inconsistently depending on who's handling the case.
  • Nothing moves forward until someone remembers to push it.

At low volume this is manageable. As the firm grows, administrative overhead grows with it — consuming time that should be spent on casework.

The Solution

Workflows that run the repeatable parts automatically

TaxRes CRM lets you build multi-step workflows triggered by events that already happen in the CRM — a new lead, a status change, a signed form, a completed task, an appointment booking. When a trigger fires, the workflow runs: tasks are created, emails go out, documents are requested, staff are notified.

TaxRes CRM includes 22 built-in workflow templates covering the most common resolution sequences. Each can be customized to match your firm's process — or build your own from scratch.

Why It Matters

Consistent follow-up is a competitive advantage

Clients in a resolution case — waiting on an OIC response, monitoring an Installment Agreement, navigating Appeals — need to know their case is moving. Firms that communicate consistently and follow up reliably retain more clients and generate more referrals.

Automation handles the parts of the process that don't require professional judgment — so practitioners can spend time on analysis, strategy, and client communication that genuinely requires expertise. Role-based permissions ensure automated actions only touch appropriate records.

How It Works

Set a trigger. Define the actions. Let it run.

Each workflow starts with a trigger and executes a sequence of actions. Workflows can run once or repeat on a schedule.

Example: New OIC Engagement
1Send welcome email and engagement letter
2Create task: collect financial documents
3Request Form 433-A via client portal
4Assign transcript pull to case manager
57-day follow-up if no documents received
6Notify partner when intake is complete
Templates

22 built-in workflow templates

Start with pre-built workflows for common resolution sequences — new client intake, OIC engagement, IA compliance monitoring, Penalty Abatement follow-up — and customize as needed.

Triggers

Workflows start automatically

Trigger when a lead is created, a case status changes, a form is signed, an appointment is booked, a task is completed, or on a scheduled date.

Email & SMS

Outreach that stays on schedule

Send follow-up emails and SMS at defined intervals — 3 days after intake, 7 days before a deadline, immediately after a status change — without manual coordination.

Task Creation

Work gets assigned automatically

When a workflow runs, tasks are created and assigned to the right team member. Nothing falls through because someone forgot to create it.

Document Requests

Ask for what you need at the right moment

Send document requests through the client portal automatically at the right point in the workflow — after intake, before a transcript pull, or when a case moves to a new stage.

Notifications

Keep the team informed

Notify staff when key events occur — a new lead books, a signed form returns, a client uploads a document, or a deadline is approaching.

Who Benefits

Every role in the firm gains time back

Practitioners stop managing follow-up calendars and focus on case strategy. Administrative staff stop creating the same tasks manually and handle exceptions instead. Case managers see work appear in their queue automatically when a case moves forward. Partners see consistent process across the firm instead of relying on each employee's individual habits — whether the firm handles OIC cases, Installment Agreements, Penalty Abatement, or Appeals.
