The Problem

Client information shouldn't live in five different places

Many firms unintentionally build their own system over time. Contact information lives in one application. Case notes are kept in another. Documents are stored in folders. Appointments live on a calendar. Phone calls and emails remain inside personal inboxes. Payments are tracked somewhere else.

The result is familiar:

  • Staff ask clients for information they've already provided.
  • Team members duplicate work because they can't see previous activity.
  • Documents are difficult to locate when they're needed.
  • New employees struggle to understand an existing case.
  • Partners lose visibility into what has — or hasn't — been completed.

As the firm grows, these disconnected systems become harder to manage. A client with an open Installment Agreement, a pending Notice of Federal Tax Lien, and an upcoming IRS deadline shouldn't require three different applications to understand.

The Solution

Keep the entire client relationship in one record

TaxRes CRM organizes every interaction around a single client profile. Instead of searching multiple applications, your team opens one client file to review contact information, active cases, communication history, documents, tasks, payments, notes, appointments, and IRS-related activity.

Every action becomes part of the client's history, giving anyone with appropriate access the context they need to continue the work — whether they handled the last call or are picking up a case for the first time.

Why It Matters

Better records lead to better client service

Clients shouldn't have to repeat their story every time they call. When a client phones to ask about their Offer in Compromise, the person answering should be able to open the file and immediately see the current status, the last communication, any outstanding documents, and what happens next — without putting the client on hold to search a second system.

When everyone on your team works from the same client record, conversations become more informed, handoffs between staff become smoother, and follow-up happens with confidence rather than guesswork.

How It Works

Everything starts with the client profile

Each client record in TaxRes CRM brings together the information your team uses every day. A staff member opening a client file can review the full picture — contact details, case history, every call logged, every email synced, every document uploaded, and every task assigned — without switching applications.

Client Record

One complete client profile

Store contact details, financial information, case history, and communication records in one organized client file that follows the client throughout the relationship.

Communications

Every conversation stays connected

Phone calls, emails, SMS messages, and notes become part of the client's timeline, giving staff the context they need before responding — whether the last contact was today or three months ago.

Tasks

Know what needs attention

The task sidebar keeps assignments and follow-ups attached to the client record so work doesn't disappear into separate to-do lists or get missed during handoffs.

Documents

Everything in one place

Access uploaded documents, signed forms, financial intake, and supporting files directly from the client record. Every document action writes a note to the file automatically.

Visibility

Give staff the access they need

Role-based permissions let firms control who can view sensitive client information — including cases involving Form 2848, Form 8821, or transcript details — while keeping collaboration efficient.

Growth

From prospect to long-term client

Convert new leads into active clients without creating duplicate records or losing the conversation history from the intake process.

Who Benefits

Built for every role in a resolution firm

Whether your firm consists of one practitioner or a growing team, organized client records reduce time spent searching for information and increase confidence that everyone is working from the same history.

Enrolled Agents reviewing transcripts, CPAs managing Appeals or Currently Not Collectible cases, attorneys handling collection matters, administrative staff scheduling appointments, and case managers tracking Installment Agreement compliance all benefit from having one place to review client information before taking the next step.

When a case changes hands — because a staff member is out, a matter escalates, or the firm grows — the record travels with it. Nothing is lost in the transition.
